# 💩 Poop Pal

**Poop Pal** is a pixel-art virtual pet game where you raise, feed, and evolve your very own sentient poop.

Inspired by Tamagotchi and absurd retro consoles, Poop Pal features animated menus, evolving poop companions, weird foods, drinks with strange effects, and unlockable minigames—each with unique gameplay and rewards. Every action, from eating to flushing, is visual, responsive, and surprisingly satisfying.

## 🎮 Core Features

- 🐣 **Evolving Poop**: Feed and hydrate to evolve your poop through increasingly weird forms.
- 🍽️ **Interactive Food & Drink Menus**: Rotating food and drink options, each with custom animations, sounds, and visual effects.
- ⏱️ **Timers & Cycles**: Food every 90 minutes, drinks every 20 minutes. Flush your poop to start a new cycle!
- 🎯 **Minigames**: One-button and gyroscope-based minigames like **Super Puff** unlock score-based rewards and items.
- 🔓 **Unlock System**: New foods, drinks, poop forms, music tracks, and cosmetics unlock as you play.
- 🧻 **Flushing Mechanic**: End a poop cycle by holding the main button—reset your poop, rate your progress, and start again.

## 🪜 Progression Design

- Score-based progression unlocks new content (foods, drinks, cosmetics, etc.).
- Certain minigames, foods, and effects are locked until specific poop evolutions or achievements are reached.
- Rarity system: Some items only appear under rare conditions or after major milestones.
- Visual upgrades: Even when your poop doesn’t evolve, meals still alter its appearance subtly.
- Time-based mechanics simulate day/night, affecting food availability and behavior.

## 📚 Future Features

- 🕹️ More minigames with unique mechanics (pumping, reflexes, gyroscope labyrinths…)
- 🎼 Unlockable background music and poop-specific tracks
- 📖 **Poop-Pedia**, **Food-Pedia**, and **Drink-Pedia** to track discovered evolutions and item effects
- 🎨 Cosmetic unlocks: Backgrounds, clouds, UI skins, poop stickers
- 🌙 Nighttime logic with moodier food sets and sleeping states
- ⭐ Cycle summary screen after each flush, showing ratings and unlocked rewards
