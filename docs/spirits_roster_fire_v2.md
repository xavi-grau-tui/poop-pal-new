# Fire realm: the 24 spirits, with a twist each (v2, 2026-11-04, for the user to pick)

v1 (`spirits_roster_fire_v1.md`) gave each slot an object or creature, and the designer drew it well,
but many came out generic: "a Japanese thing with a face". v2 gives every spirit a **twist**, the
thing you'd tell a friend about:
- a behaviour
- a flaw
- an unexpected combination
- a bit of real Japanese life most people don't know

**Status:**
- **keep**: approved in rounds 4–5, the twist is already in the drawing
- **redraw**: approved before, but generic; v2 changes it
- **new**: not drawn yet, or drawn but not approved

**Alt** is a second option. Pick one, edit it, or write your own.

**Fire's look:** ember red, ash white, lantern-paper cream and gold, with some green (shishito, the hoozuki's leaves) and storm lavender for contrast. Every body must stand out against the shrine's dark wood.

**Faces:** no default kawaii face. No pink blush ovals and no big round shiny eyes unless the spirit's twist calls for them. Each face below is part of the idea.
