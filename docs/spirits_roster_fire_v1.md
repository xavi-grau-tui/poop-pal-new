# Fire realm: the 24 spirits' ideas (draft for the user to pick, 2026-11-04)

The style anchor is the October sheets: `docs/mockups/pal_brainstorm/current_spirit_style_reference.png`
and `spirit_30_alternatives/alternative_c.png`. They have chunky round bodies, soft glossy shading,
expressive lidded eyes and one Japanese idea each. Here they are in the shrine:
`docs/mockups/spirits/round3_review/october_style_in_home.png`.

Each slot keeps its id and its place in the tree (`docs/kami_spirits_tree.md`). The idea and name are
new. **Alt** is a second option for the slot. Pick one, edit it, or write your own.

Fire: ember red, soot black, lantern-paper cream, gold. Keep the bodies light enough to stand out
against the shrine's dark wood.
