# RC Prix · Lagoon, round 1b: style first, then paint at 4×

Thank you for the first sample (`round1_sample_preview.png`). The sizes, hard pixels and palette
direction are right, but **the user's verdict is that it's not there yet**, and the numbers agree.
This round changes how we work, so it gets to the Garden's level.

| Lagoon `water_a`, `channel_a`, `shallows_a`, `sand_a` | 4–5 | 1–2% |

- The decor is fine as a start but simple.

## The new way of working (what made the Garden work)

### Step 1: style pictures, the user picks one

Make **2 or 3 pictures of a Lagoon course at sunset in the Garden's style**, with your image tool:
a channel of water marked by red and yellow buoys, sand banks, shallows, a sandy spit between two
curves, open water with an island and palms, the toy boat. Use `reference/garden_quality_bar_*.png`
and `reference/garden_course_preview.png` as the style and detail target: the same pixel-art look,
the same amount of texture per area, the same camera (from above, slight angle).

Vary them, for example: one calm and glassy with long reflections, one with more visible wave
crests and foam, one with clearer turquoise shallows over the sand within the sunset palette.

**The user picks one before any game file is made.** It becomes `reference/lagoon_style.png`, the
target for everything after.

### Step 2: paint at 4×, we convert

Paint every piece **at 4× its game size** to match the chosen picture, and we bring it down to the
game grid with a script (`tools/art/rcprix_lagoon_from_paint.py`), then check it in the game. This
is how the barriers worked first time.

| Piece | Game size | Paint at (4×) | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each square (water, channel, shallows, sand) | 190×190 | 760×760 | `round1b_paint/water_a_4x.png` ... |
| Islands | 90×50 to 180×70 | 4× that | `round1b_paint/island_b_4x.png` ... |
| Palms | about 30×40 | 120×160 | `round1b_paint/palm_a_4x.png` |
| Decor | 8–16 px | 4× that | `round1b_paint/starfish_4x.png` ... |
| Bank sample | over `templates/2_bank_guide_4x.png` | as is | `round1_bank/bank_paint_4x.png` |

- **One game pixel = one 4×4 block.** Paint in 4×4 blocks (a 4 px grid in your app helps). Where a
  block has mixed colours, the converter takes its most common one, so soft brushes and anti-aliasing
  turn into noise. Paint hard-edged, block by block.
- **Detail like the Garden:** grass blades, leaf clusters and flowers in the Garden are 2–6 game px
  each. Wave crests, ripples, sand grains, shell bits and foam should be the same size, and there
  should be about as many of them per area as the Garden's grass has.
- **Squares still tile:** paint each 760×760 so it wraps (left edge continues the right, top the
  bottom), and keep the outer 12 px (3 game px) identical in A, B and C of a kind. The converter
  checks both.
- **The bank sample is a painting, not the guide:** paint over the guide's bands. The lines only
  show where the bands are; don't keep them as art. What we need to see:
  - foam breaking where the shallows meet the sand, and wet darker sand just past it,
  - the sand bank with its texture,
  - the edge where the sand meets the open water,
  - the channel's edge **without** a dark line (the buoys mark it); at most a slight change of tone.
- **Palms seen at the game's slight angle**, like the Garden's trees: a trunk leaning a little, the
  crown's leaves drooping, lit from the top left, not a flat star.
- **No script-drawn art.** Scripts are fine for checking and for exporting, not for making the
  picture.
