# Arcade Cabinet v7 final_c

Approved final_b outline and proportions with an ImageGen material-finish pass, separated for the game's depth layers. The full ImageGen painting is `source_imagegen.png`; `tools/art/hokora_arcade_v7_final_c_export.swift` places it on the approved 220-pixel grid and derives the registered layers. The cabinet keeps the approved sign, joystick, buttons, coin grille, and flared pillars.

## Files and draw order

1. `inside.png` — v6.2-style navy/plum screen recess and cream/red bevel. It extends six pixels past the 80 × 78 aperture for tilt.
2. `front.png` — cabinet and deck, with a transparent screen opening and red pillars continuing behind the wings.
3. Game-created depth for the two trays.
4. `trays.png` — the two approved wing faces, in their original positions.
5. Game-created ornaments on the trays. The offering table remains game-created in the empty centre of the deck.

`guide.png` marks the high left bell hook in orange, two lantern hooks in yellow, and the centres of the tray tops in green. `preview_8x.png` shows the assembled layers; `icon.png` is 96 × 96. The cabinet layers are 220 × 220. All layer edges are fully opaque or fully transparent.
