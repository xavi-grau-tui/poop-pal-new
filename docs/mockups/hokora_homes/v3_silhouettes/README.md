# New Homes v3: silhouette review

Nine distinct **empty home frames** for shape selection. Each `_imagegen.png` is a separate ImageGen concept. The matching `_110.png` is that concept reduced with nearest-neighbour sampling to a 110 × 110 transparent canvas and made into a single `#200010` silhouette. `_preview_8x.png` is the same 110-pixel silhouette enlarged without smoothing on a cream background. `all_nine_silhouettes.png` arranges those nine previews for comparison; the sheet adds no art.

These are **silhouette concepts**, not game-ready homes. No table, lantern, bell, shelf object, spirit, sky or background was added. After shape selection, the colour concepts need exact guide alignment for the window, floor line, beam, hooks and shelf spots. The final layers must be cut from each approved coloured front.

Prompt direction used for each ImageGen concept: “Create one empty-frame building in solid dark plum on transparency, with a bold distinct outer silhouette, clear central spirit window and floor, room for the hanging beam and separate movable decorations. Keep its unique building profile; omit the spirit, offering table, lanterns, bell, shelves and background.” Each home then received its specific subject and shape direction from the table above.
