# Mountain, Moon, Fox, River: the 96 spirits (draft, 2026-11-06)

This follows the method of the Fire realm (`docs/spirits_roster_fire.md`, done) and the style bible (`docs/kami_spirits_bible.md`).

**Each realm's sheets, in `docs/mockups/spirits/october/`:**
- `<realm>_template_for_designer.png`: the October originals on their cream, with the gap cells blank
- `<realm>_tree_proposal.png`: the tree at 4x, the gaps marked

**The grid:** 6 columns × 4 rows of 260 px cells, 1560 × 1040 in total.
- **Columns 1–5:** the five lines, with the kid on top and its three adults below (the Pure or Ultra adult first).
- **Column 6:** the baby, the Tech mutant, the Cosmic mutant and the legend.

**Codes:**
- A, B or C + row + column = a cell of `pal_brainstorm/spirit_30_alternatives/alternative_*.png`
- R + number = a sprite of `current_spirit_style_reference.png`

## Shared rules for the gaps

- **Everything in `kami_spirits_bible.md`:**
  - one low round body sitting on the floor, no arms
  - the small calm face, low on the body
  - one Japanese thing each, 1–2 features
  - native pixel art matching the neighbours
- **Tech mutants:** the same wooden wind-up spirit as Fire's (`october_fire/fire_filled.png`, column 6, row 2), with a brass key on its back and brass gear cheeks, lacquered in the realm's colour, plus one realm touch.
- **Cosmic mutants:** a sprinkle of tiny twinkling stars on and around them.
- **Legends:** the biggest on the sheet (about 1.3 × the others). Grand but calm, still the same face.
- **Contrast:** bodies light enough to stand out against the shrine's dark wood. No black bodies.
